tensorless is built in the open, and the people running it on their own cards are a big part of it. Here is where to find everyone.

## Discord

**[Join the tensorless Discord](https://discord.gg/3Qs2uejUf9).** It is the place for:

- questions about setting up a host, a driver or a container runtime;
- results from your own P100, good or bad;
- ideas for models, cards and kernels.

## GitHub

The repository is [iammrduncan/tensorless](https://github.com/iammrduncan/tensorless).

- **[Issues](https://github.com/iammrduncan/tensorless/issues)** are for problems and concrete requests: a command that fails, a number that does not match its receipt, a wrong page in these docs, a missing credit.
- **[Releases](https://github.com/iammrduncan/tensorless/releases)** have the launcher archives and each release's notes.

When you report a problem, include what `tensorless version` and `tensorless doctor` print, and the full command you ran. [Fix a failed check or start](../how-to/use/troubleshoot.md) covers the common ones first.

## Share your numbers

A speed measured on someone else's P100 is the best check there is on a receipt. If you run `tensorless bench`, post the result on Discord or in an issue, with your card, driver and launcher version. [Share your results](../how-to/use/share-results.md) says what to include.
